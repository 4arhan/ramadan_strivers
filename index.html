📘 How to Use This App

Everything you need to know — tap any section to expand it

📋 Daily Checklist ▶
You get 60+ daily deeds organised into 7 categories — Salah, Quran, Character, Charity, Avoidance, Abu Bakr Challenge, and Last 10 Nights. Tap any category to open it, then tick off what you've done. Your daily percentage updates as you go. Each category starts closed so you can focus on one area at a time.
📖 Quran Tracker ▶
Track your journey through all 604 pages of the Quran. After each reading session, enter your pages and tap "+ Add" — pages add up throughout the day. The dashboard shows your total progress, which Juz you're on, and how many pages per day you need to complete the Quran by Day 30. A chart shows your actual pace vs the ideal pace so you know if you're on track.
🔥 Streaks ▶
The app tracks four consecutive-day streaks: Fajr at the Masjid, Isha at the Masjid, daily Quran, and giving Sadaqah. Each day you keep it up, your count grows. At milestones like 3, 7, 10, and 20 days, you'll see a congratulations message pop up. It's a simple way to build consistency in worship.
🏅 12 Badges ▶
Unlock achievements as you progress through Ramadan:

🌱 First Step — do anything on Day 1
📅 Week One — log all 7 days of the first week
🎯 Halfway — stay active for the first 15 days
☀️ Fajr Warrior — 7-day Fajr at Masjid streak
🌙 Tahajjud Master — pray Tahajjud on 7 nights
📖 Quran Lover — read Quran on 15 days
💎 Generous — give charity on 10 days
🥇 Abu Bakr — complete the full challenge in one day
🏆 Abu Bakr Gold — do it on 3 different days
🌟 Last 10 Hero — avg 70%+ across the Last 10 Nights
✨ Perfectionist — hit 100% on 3 days
👑 Champion — score 60%+ on 20 days

Print your PDF report on Eid and use the badges to celebrate and reward your kids!
🌙 Last 10 Nights & Laylatul Qadr ▶
A countdown to the Last 10 Nights begins on Day 15. When Day 21 arrives, a bonus worship category unlocks with extra deeds like I'tikaf, long sujood, and extra Quran. Odd nights (21, 23, 25, 27, 29) are highlighted across the app to remind you — any one of them could be Laylatul Qadr.
⭐ Abu Bakr Challenge ▶
The Prophet ﷺ once asked his companions four questions: "Who among you is fasting today? Who followed a funeral? Who fed someone? Who visited someone who is sick?" Abu Bakr (RA) was the only one who said "I did" to all four. This challenge asks you to do the same in a single day. When you've completed 3 out of 4, the app encourages you to go for all four.
🌸 Sisters' Menstrual Cycle ▶
Activate the toggle on the Today tab during your cycle. Salah and fasting items will be greyed out and excluded from your percentage. This way, your score reflects only the deeds that apply to you — so you can still aim for 100%.
📊 Progress & Charts ▶
The Progress tab gives you a full picture of your Ramadan. You'll find a colour-coded heatmap showing your consistency across all 30 days, a calendar you can tap to see each day's details, a weekly comparison chart, a breakdown by Ramadan thirds (Mercy, Forgiveness, Salvation), a daily trend line, and a pie chart showing how your effort is split across categories. Sections are collapsible — open what interests you.
🧠 Flash Quiz ▶
Test what you've learned! The Quiz tab turns every hadith and ayah in this app into true-or-false flashcards, organised into six categories — Fasting, Salah & Dhikr, Quran, Character, Charity, and the Last 10 Nights. Each round is 10 shuffled cards: answer, watch the card flip, and see the correct answer with its source on sunnah.com or quran.com. Your accuracy and best score per category are saved, so keep playing until you master them all.
🕌 New to Islam or Learning? ▶
Every deed in the checklist has a hadith reference and a short explanation behind it. Turn on "Detail Mode" (in Settings below, or on the Today tab) and you'll see the source and meaning of each item. You don't need any prior knowledge — the app teaches you as you use it.
📄 PDF Report ▶
Download a printable PDF of your entire Ramadan journey — your daily completion, Quran pages read, badges unlocked, and best streaks. It's a meaningful keepsake, and a great way to celebrate your children's achievements on Eid day.
🔒 Privacy & Data ▶
All your data lives on your device only. There are no accounts, no servers, and no tracking. Nothing ever leaves your phone. You can export a backup file anytime and import it on another device. The app works completely offline.
