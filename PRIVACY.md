# Privacy — Ramadan Strivers

**Default: everything stays on your device.** Deeds, Quran pages, badges, journal and settings are stored in your browser's local storage. No account is needed. Usage statistics (Google Analytics) count which screens are opened and how often a few features are used (for example a deed ticked, a city set, a competition joined), plus the app language, mode and theme. They never include your name, email, journal, which deeds you ticked or your coordinates. Statistics cookies are off in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland until you allow them, you can turn statistics off in Me → Settings, and they are always off in kids mode. Advertising features are disabled.

**If you create an account (optional):** we store your email, an encrypted password hash (or nothing, with magic-link sign-in), and a JSON snapshot of your tracker data so it can sync between your devices. You can delete the account and all cloud data in one tap (More → Account → Delete). Deletion is immediate and permanent.

**Competitions:** a participant shares only a display name, an optional class/team label, and aggregate scores (points, days logged, average %, Quran pages). The organiser can see per-day totals for integrity review — never which individual deeds were ticked. Participants can leave at any time; organisers can remove anyone. Closing a competition stops all score updates.

**Competitions, extras:** Other things a competition can hold, only when used: the dates you checked in at an event with the organiser's QR code, your display name on a sign-up list you chose to join, and the email addresses of the colleagues an organiser adds to help run it. A record of the changes organisers make (who, what, when) is kept so mistakes can be traced. An organisation's public page and league tables show totals and class or team names only, never a participant's name.

**Buddies:** if you create a buddy link, the server stores the name you typed and a short summary (your Ramadan day, today's completion %, your best current streak, days logged, Quran pages and the date you were last active). Anyone who has the link can see that summary and send you one of four preset encouragements with their name; they cannot send free text. Individual deeds, the journal and sisters' mode are never shared. "Stop sharing" deletes the link and its data from the server.

**Family profiles and the home display:** profiles are kept only on your device; nothing about them is sent to the server. The home display reads the data already on the device. An embedded leaderboard on an organiser's website shows the same public board as the app (display names, teams, points) and sets no cookies.

**The live demo:** the demo on our product page is the app running on sample data. It does not read or change anything you have saved in the app, creates no account, sends no statistics, and keeps what you do in that browser tab only, until you close it. If you search for a city there, the typed text is sent to our server as in the app.

**Children (under 13):** no email, no account and no phone number is ever required of a participant. Organisers running school competitions should enable *Participants under 13* in competition settings: this hides social-sharing buttons for those participants and keeps display names only. Schools remain the data controller for their rosters; we recommend first names + class, not full names, and deleting the competition after Eid (one tap).

**Push notifications:** stored as a browser subscription endpoint plus your chosen hour and, if you enabled prayer times, your approximate coordinates (for iftar reminders). Turn off in Settings to delete the subscription.

**Prayer times:** your location (a chosen city, or coordinates rounded to about 1 km) is stored on your device, and leaves it only with push reminders or, when signed in, your synced settings. Times are calculated on the device. A city search sends only the typed text and app language to our server, which asks the Open-Meteo geocoding service (GeoNames data); major cities are searched offline, and "Use my location" is matched to the nearest city on the device.

**AI features (when the server enables them):** the deed label, its cited source, your language and reading level are sent to the AI provider to generate an explanation; your name and tracker data are not. Explanations are cached and are never religious rulings.

**WhatsApp bot (when enabled):** your phone number is stored to link messages to your competition entry; message content is not kept beyond what you logged.
