# Privacy — Ramadan Strivers

**Default: everything stays on your device.** Deeds, Quran pages, badges, journal and settings are stored in your browser's local storage. No account is needed. No analytics beyond basic page views (Google Analytics on the public site; none inside the installed app flows).

**If you create an account (optional):** we store your email, an encrypted password hash (or nothing, with magic-link sign-in), and a JSON snapshot of your tracker data so it can sync between your devices. You can delete the account and all cloud data in one tap (More → Account → Delete). Deletion is immediate and permanent.

**Competitions:** a participant shares only a display name, an optional class/team label, and aggregate scores (points, days logged, average %, Quran pages). The organiser can see per-day totals for integrity review — never which individual deeds were ticked. Participants can leave at any time; organisers can remove anyone. Closing a competition stops all score updates.

**Children (under 13):** no email, no account and no phone number is ever required of a participant. Organisers running school competitions should enable *Participants under 13* in competition settings: this hides social-sharing buttons for those participants and keeps display names only. Schools remain the data controller for their rosters; we recommend first names + class, not full names, and deleting the competition after Eid (one tap).

**Push notifications:** stored as a browser subscription endpoint plus your chosen hour and, if you enabled prayer times, your approximate coordinates (for iftar reminders). Turn off in Settings to delete the subscription.

**Prayer times:** your location is stored only on your device unless you enable push reminders.

**AI features (when the server enables them):** the deed label, its cited source, your language and reading level are sent to the AI provider to generate an explanation; your name and tracker data are not. Explanations are cached and are never religious rulings.

**WhatsApp bot (when enabled):** your phone number is stored to link messages to your competition entry; message content is not kept beyond what you logged.
