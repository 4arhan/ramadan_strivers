Privacy

By default everything stays on your device. Deeds, Quran pages, badges, journal and settings are stored in your browser's local storage. No account is needed. No analytics beyond basic page views (Google Analytics on the public site; none inside the installed app flows).

If you create an account (optional)

We store your email, a securely hashed password (or no password at all, with magic-link sign-in), and a copy of your app data so it can sync between your devices: your days, Quran log, badges, journal, learning and Ramadan Prep progress, and your settings (including your prayer-times location, if you set one). You can delete the account and all cloud data in one tap (Me → Account → Delete account and cloud data). Deletion is immediate and permanent; the data on your device is kept.

Competitions

A participant shares only a display name, an optional class or team label, and aggregate scores (points, days logged, average percentage, Quran pages). The organiser can see per-day totals for integrity review, never which individual deeds were ticked. Participants can leave at any time; organisers can remove anyone. Closing a competition stops all score updates.

Buddies

If you create a buddy link, the server stores the name you typed and a short summary: your Ramadan day, today's completion, your best current streak, days logged, Quran pages and the date you were last active. Anyone who has the link can see that summary and send you one of four preset encouragements with their name; they cannot send free text. Individual deeds, the journal and sisters' mode are never shared. "Stop sharing" deletes the link and its data from the server.

Family profiles, the home display and embedded leaderboards

Family profiles are kept only on your device; nothing about them is sent to the server. The home display reads the data already on the device. A leaderboard embedded on an organiser's website shows the same public board as the app (display names, teams, points) and sets no cookies.

Children (under 13)

No email, no account and no phone number is ever required of a participant. Organisers running school competitions should enable Participants under 13 in the competition settings: this hides social-sharing buttons for those participants and keeps display names only. Schools remain the data controller for their rosters; we recommend first names and class rather than full names, and deleting the competition after Eid (one tap).

Push notifications

Stored as a browser subscription endpoint plus your chosen hour, your time zone and language and, if you set prayer times, your approximate coordinates (for iftar reminders). Turn them off in Me → Settings to delete the subscription. Resetting everything on a device also turns them off there.

Prayer times

Your location is stored on your device. It leaves the device only with push reminders (see above) or, when you are signed in, as part of your synced settings.

AI features (when the server enables them)

Explain: the deed's label, its short explanation and cited source, your language and reading level are sent to the AI provider to generate an explanation.

Reflection prompts: when you tap Suggest a question in the journal, a summary of the previous day is sent to generate a question: its day number, completion percentage, Quran pages, Fajr and Quran streaks, and which deeds were done or missed. Your journal text is never sent.

Quiz: for the kids and advanced quiz levels, the quiz statements (not your answers) are sent to be reworded.

Your name and email are never sent. Responses are cached and are never religious rulings.

WhatsApp bot (when enabled)

Your phone number is stored to link messages to your competition entry; message content is not kept beyond what you logged.

Your data on this device

Me → Your data lets you export a backup file, import one (it replaces only the days, entries and settings it contains), or delete everything the app stores on this device.
